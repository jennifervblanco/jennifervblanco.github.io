About

Marketing found me through a camera.

I started by building my own brand behind a lens. Somewhere between the shot list and the sales funnel, I realized the part I loved was not the picture. It was the way the right story made people want something.

How I work

I make decisions on evidence and taste, and I do not think those are opposites. A number tells you what happened; judgment tells you what to do about it. I want both in the room.

I have run a profit and loss, answered the messages, and shipped the work, so I treat every brand as if the outcome is mine. I am most useful where a team needs one person who can set the direction and also produce the work, without losing the plan in a hand-off.

What I'm looking for

A team building a consumer brand with a real point of view, where marketing drives the business rather than decorates it. I care most about the industries that shape how people live, eat, travel, move, and celebrate.

I am finishing an MBA at San Diego State to sharpen the business side of the craft, and I am building toward leading brand and marketing for a company people love.
